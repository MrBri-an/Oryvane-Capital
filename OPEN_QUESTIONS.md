# Oryvane Capital Open Questions

These decisions require client confirmation before the related implementation phase.

## 1. Branding

1. Is Oryvane Capital the final registered brand name?
2. What is the final logo direction?
3. What primary domain will be used?
4. Which countries will the platform serve?

## 2. Investment Plans

1. What investment products will be available at launch?
2. What are the minimum and maximum amounts?
3. What durations will be offered?
4. Are returns fixed, variable or manually entered?
5. Can users cancel active investments?
6. What happens when an investment matures?

## 3. Currencies

1. Which fiat currencies will be supported?
2. Will balances be held separately by currency?
3. Will the platform display exchange rates?
4. How should Bitcoin deposits be converted for display?

## 4. Bank Payments

1. Which bank account details will be displayed?
2. Will one account or multiple accounts be used?
3. Can admin change active payment details?
4. What proof is mandatory?
5. What is the expected review time?

## 5. Bitcoin Payments

1. Which Bitcoin address will be used?
2. Will one static address or generated addresses be used?
3. How many confirmations are required?
4. Will the credited value use the sent BTC amount or a fiat conversion?
5. What exchange-rate source and timestamp will be used?

## 6. Returns and Earnings

1. Who is authorised to add earnings?
2. How often are earnings updated?
3. Are earnings withdrawable immediately?
4. Can earnings be corrected or reversed?
5. Should users receive email notifications for every update?

## 7. Withdrawals

1. What is the minimum withdrawal amount?
2. Are there withdrawal fees?
3. How long should review take?
4. Are new withdrawal destinations subject to a delay?
5. Can users cancel pending withdrawals?
6. When should funds be reserved or deducted?

## 8. Fees

1. Are deposit fees charged?
2. Are management fees charged?
3. Are early cancellation fees charged?
4. How should fees appear in the dashboard?

## 9. Account Restrictions

1. Can restricted users still view balances?
2. Can suspended users sign in?
3. What actions remain available to blocked users?
4. Should restrictions expire automatically?
5. Is an appeal or support process required?

## 10. Notifications

1. Which email provider will be used?
2. Which events require email and in-app notifications?
3. Can users disable non-security notifications?
4. Should admins be notified about every new payment and withdrawal?

## 11. Legal Content

1. Who will provide the Terms and Conditions?
2. Who will provide the Privacy Policy?
3. Who will provide the Risk Disclosure?
4. What company information must be displayed?
5. Which jurisdiction governs the agreement?

## 12. Administrator Roles

1. How many administrators are expected at launch?
2. Which roles will be used initially?
3. Is dual approval required for large financial adjustments?
4. What amount should trigger extra approval?
5. Which administrators can export financial reports?
6. How long should admin sessions remain active?
